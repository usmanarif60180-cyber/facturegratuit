Privacy Policy / Politique de confidentialité

Last updated: 02 August 2026. This policy explains how ProFacture AI handles personal data, invoice data, analytics and advertising cookies.

Data entered in invoices and quotes

Information such as customer name, address, SIRET/SIREN, vehicle VIN, registration plate, product lines, prices and notes is used to generate invoices, quotes and PDFs. The application is designed to process document data in the browser and, when the user is logged in, to save workspace data to the user's own account storage.

We do not sell customer data, invoice data, SIRET numbers, vehicle numbers or business documents.

Account and authentication

Authentication is provided through Firebase Authentication. Login providers may include email/password, Google, Apple or phone OTP depending on the enabled settings. Account identifiers are used only to connect a user to their own workspace data.

Analytics and advertising

The site may use Google Analytics, Google AdSense, Adsterra or similar advertising/measurement partners. These services can use cookies or similar technologies to measure traffic, prevent abuse, personalize ads where consent allows it, and improve the service.

Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your account data by emailing contact@facturergratuit.com. You can also remove locally saved browser data from your device and delete saved workspace items from the dashboard where available.

Security summary

  • Account-based access for private dashboard data.
  • Firebase-backed authentication.
  • No selling of invoice or customer data.
  • User-controlled document history and storage cleanup.
  • Cookie consent and privacy controls for advertising partners.

Security

Security and account protection

ProFacture AI is designed for business documents, invoices, quotes, clients, products and dashboard history. Security is part of the product architecture.

Authentication

User accounts are protected with Firebase Authentication. Users should keep passwords private, use trusted devices and sign out on shared computers.

Private dashboard data

Dashboard data such as clients, products, invoices, projects and company profile is intended to be scoped to the logged-in user's workspace. Public visitors cannot access private account views without login.

Storage limits and cleanup

To keep cloud usage manageable, storage limits and optional cleanup flows can be used for saved files and history. Users should delete old documents they no longer need.

Reporting security issues

If you find a vulnerability or suspicious behavior, please email contact@facturergratuit.com with details and steps to reproduce.

Security checklist

  • Use HTTPS only.
  • Keep Firebase security rules strict.
  • Never expose private tokens in public code.
  • Validate uploads and limit storage usage.
  • Use account-level access for saved business data.

Invoices

How to Write an Invoice That Gets Paid Faster

The details that make clients pay on time — from clear payment terms to the exact information an invoice needs to avoid delays.

TI
ProFacture AI Team
May 04, 2026 · 4 min read
SHARE

Late payments are rarely about a client refusing to pay — they're usually about friction. A confusing invoice, a missing due date, or an unclear payment method can push your invoice to the bottom of someone's inbox.

Put the essentials where they can't be missed

  • A clear invoice number, so both sides can reference it in emails
  • The exact amount due, in a large, unambiguous font
  • A due date — not just "payment terms: Net 30" buried in fine print
  • Your payment details right on the document

Set payment terms before the work starts

The best time to agree on payment terms is before you start the project, not when the invoice lands. State the due date, any late fees, and accepted payment methods in your quote or contract.

💡 Shorter terms get paid faster. "Due on receipt" or "Net 7" consistently outperforms "Net 30" for small invoices.

Make the invoice itself easy to trust

Include your business name, address and tax/VAT number, and keep the design clean and consistent every time. Clients pay faster when an invoice looks like it came from an established business.

Free Invoice Generator for Germany

German invoices (Rechnungen) have specific mandatory fields under the UStG. Missing one is the most common reason a German client's accounting team rejects an invoice.

Create a Germany invoice — free View invoice template
Currency
EUR (€)
Consumption tax
MwSt / USt (VAT)

Business requirements

  • A Steuernummer (tax number) or USt-IdNr (VAT ID) depending on registration
  • Confirmation of whether you qualify for the Kleinunternehmer VAT exemption
  • A registered business address

What a compliant invoice needs

  • A unique, sequential invoice number
  • Full name and address of both parties
  • Net amount, VAT rate, VAT amount and gross total shown separately
⚠️ General overview, not tax advice. Confirm current MwSt rates with a Steuerberater or the Bundeszentralamt für Steuern.
