# FacturerGratuit business platform audit (2026-10-04)

## Scope and evidence

Static SPA (`index.html`, `workspace-ui.js/css`), financial engine, Firestore rules, Cloud Functions, and the existing 16 regression checks were inspected. The local public site was smoke-tested in Chrome at desktop (1440px), tablet (768px), and mobile (390px) in light and dark themes. No authenticated Firebase account was available in this local run, so private reads/writes and cross-user collaboration were not verified end to end. The repository `build` script is a no-op; compilation success is not evidence of workflow correctness.

## Architectural risk and next phase

Personal records under `users/{uid}` and collaborative records under `companies/{companyId}` are separate data models. Wiring the team screen directly to the callable invitations would not make existing projects, invoices, or tasks shared. A safe next phase needs a per-company migration plan, backups, membership-aware reads/writes, project/task role tests in the Firebase emulator, and a controlled rollout. Do not claim team collaboration complete until two distinct accounts can work on the same project while unauthorized users are denied by rules.

Before production release, verify signed-in create/edit/preview/PDF for invoice and quote; company switching; client/CRM/project/task CRUD; AI failure/retry; all loading/error/empty states; roles and permissions; and cloud restore. Test each at desktop, tablet, and mobile in both themes. Deploy and test the Firestore rule separately from the UI changes.
