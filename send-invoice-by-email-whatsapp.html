How to send an invoice by email or WhatsApp

A professional PDF is only useful when the client receives it clearly. Use a short message, attach the PDF, include the total, due date and payment method, then follow up from your invoice dashboard.

Recommended sending workflow

1. Check the PDF previewConfirm logo, company details, client name, address, line items, tax and total before downloading.
2. Use a clear subjectExample: Invoice INV-2026-001 - Your Company. This makes it searchable in email and accounting software.
3. Include the total and payment detailsMention the total amount, currency, due date and bank details or payment instructions.
4. Save the client and productSave repeat clients and services so the next invoice takes less than a minute.
5. Track statusUse draft, sent, viewed, partially paid, paid and overdue statuses to keep your cash flow visible.
